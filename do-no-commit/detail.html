About the workshop

Overview

A full-day, hands-on session on using Claude to automate documentation, summarize meetings, generate business reports, and build repeatable AI-powered workflows for everyday work.

Learning outcomes

  • Draft and refine prompts for real business tasks
  • Automate meeting summaries and email drafts
  • Build a reusable, shareable Claude Project

Who this is for

Operations, marketing, and business teams who want practical AI skills — no coding experience required.

Laptop (Windows or Mac)

Sign up beforehand so you can jump straight into exercises.
