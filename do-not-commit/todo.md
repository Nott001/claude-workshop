# Unify table designs so they're consistent

Tables are used by staff (facilitators and admins), thus their designs should look like a "power-user" and not cluttered. Additionally, we should add search options and filtering.
Clicking an entry should also display a drawer, rather than having the actions inside of the table itself. It could also be other information, like the filter itself, etc. It's all dependent on the final
design output for tables.

## Context

All of our table designs are different, from the kiosk, to the attendee table in the event details, to the organization and logs. We want to unify them to keep it consistent while also making them look
"power-user" friendly. Our goal isn't to make it look good for users but to bring the most functionality without clutter.
